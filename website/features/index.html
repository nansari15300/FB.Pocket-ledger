App features

What you get inside Pocket Ledger (web, Windows EXE, and Android APK).

  1. Multi-company booksCreate and switch companies without mixing ledgers.
  2. Sale vouchersInvoice sales with party, items, tax and narration.
  3. Purchase vouchersRecord supplier bills and purchase returns flow.
  4. ReceiptReceive money against parties and open bills.
  5. PaymentPay suppliers and settle outstanding purchases.
  6. ContraMove funds between cash and bank accounts.
  7. Journal entriesManual debit/credit adjustments when needed.
  8. Inter-company vouchersTransfer between your own companies with linked books.
  9. Bill-wise linkingAllocate receipts/payments to specific invoices.
  10. Edit lock when linkedLinked vouchers stay protected so allocations stay correct.
  11. Party mastersCustomers and suppliers with contact and balance views.
  12. Party ledgerRunning balance and voucher history per party.
  13. Bank & cash accountsTrack multiple bank and cash books in one place.
  14. Staff mastersKeep staff records for salary and operations.
  15. Tax setupConfigure tax rates used on vouchers and reports.
  16. Items / stock catalogMaintain items for sale and purchase lines.
  17. DaybookSee today’s (or any day’s) vouchers in one list.
  18. Dashboard KPIsSale, purchase, payment and cash movement at a glance.
  19. Reports suiteLedgers, daybook and business reports from the same data.
  20. Voucher search & filtersFind entries by party, type, date or voucher number.
  21. Attachment uploadAttach bills, scans and photos to vouchers.
  22. Camera capture (mobile)Snap documents on phone and link them to entries.
  23. Image previewOpen attachments clearly without leaving the voucher.
  24. PDF portal previewSharp first-page PDF preview in the attachment portal.
  25. Attachment galleryBrowse company attachments in one place.
  26. Local-first dataBooks stay usable on device; sync is optional.
  27. Online company syncLive Firebase sync for companies you keep in the cloud.
  28. Cloud attachment syncStorage-backed files for online companies.
  29. PL Server (LAN share)Share selected companies on your local network.
  30. PL Server live deltaHost and clients stay updated with change-only sync.
  31. Backup & restore (.plbp)Portable company backup with attachments.
  32. Auto backup schedulerSchedule recurring local backups from settings.
  33. Windows EXE installDesktop installer for offline-friendly office use.
  34. Android APKNative Android build for shop-floor and field entry.
  35. Web app under /appBrowser access with the same product surface.
  36. Release update checkEXE/APK can prompt when a newer published build exists.
  37. Versioned downloadsPick latest or recent prior EXE/APK from Downloads.
  38. Settings & preferencesCompany, sync, backup and display controls in one area.
